# Fieldwork Clinical and clinical templates

Jake works in clinical and recovery services in Massachusetts. His income projects include niche clinical/counseling templates (PDF drafts), the Fieldwork Clinical curriculum (inpatient track A01-A12, outpatient track B01-B10, facilitator + participant versions), and a static SEO site. Products stay DRAFTS until Jake approves (selling = PIN).

## Must-haves
- Every clinical or safety handout includes: **"If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US)".**
- Overdose/naloxone wording: call 911 first, then give naloxone. Warn that tolerance drops after abstinence (higher overdose risk on return to use).
- No PHI, no client stories that could identify anyone, no dosing advice.
- Cite sources for factual claims (SAMHSA, MA regulations). Verify regulation section numbers before using them (e.g. 105 CMR 164 sections). Mark anything unsourced.

## Massachusetts terms to get right
- CSS = Clinical Stabilization Services; ATS = Acute Treatment Services.
- MGL ch. 94G is cannabis, not LADC licensing.
- Don't use "DEAR MAN" or other proprietary DBT skill names in products; describe the skill generically.
- Check the MA Good Samaritan law against the statute before stating it.

## AI routing
- **Never send clinical, legal, or customer-facing Fieldwork Clinical text to Gemini** (free tier may train on it, and in testing it missed domain errors such as the overdose-after-abstinence line).
- Drafting can be done by the rig's local model (private, on Jake's hardware); Claude or Jake does the clinical review.
- Gemini is fine only for typos and generic tech docs.
