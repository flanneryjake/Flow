# Groceries and meal plans

## Rules
- **Never Walmart** (Jake, 2026-10-01: a ~$85 list became $223).
- Default: build an **Instacart** cart (Instacart connector: search_products, then cart) so he doesn't shop in person. Keep the total **including fees** inside the budget.
- Budget: groceries + household $400/month (~$90/week). Weekly can float; the MONTH must hold, because savings go to paying off his credit card.
- Stores near Plymouth, MA 02360: **ALDI, 246 Colony Pl** (cheapest, ~28% below average; carts need a quarter; weekly ad runs Wed-Tue, aldiweekly.com has it as text), then **Market Basket #80, 1 Commerce Way** (~15% below). Stop & Shop is the fallback.
- He cooks simple, cheap meals for one. He works Tue-Sat and gets up around 4 AM, so breakfasts must be grab-and-go.

## His regular basket (plan with it, suggest cheaper swaps)
Coffee, energy drinks (Lucky Energy 12-pack), a 12-pack drink (~$20), Liquid Death sparkling water, Fairlife milk (regular + chocolate), Rao's sauce, organic eggs, pizza, whey protein, Teddie peanut butter, Oats Overnight packets / Quaker instant oatmeal. Brita 10-cup pitcher (filter due about every 3 months; last one 2026-10-01).

## Meal plans
- Start from what's already in the kitchen (ask for a photo or list if you don't know) and only buy fresh items to fill gaps.
- Format: "What you have", one ~1 hour prep session (batch rice in broth, brown all the ground beef and split it taco/pasta, roast potatoes + veg, cook quinoa), then a day-by-day table of Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner, then a short fresh-items cart.
- Food safety: raw ground beef 1-2 days in the fridge, cooked food 4 days.
- A daily 3 PM "meal prep" email gives that day's recipes + prep from the newest plan; when the plan runs out, write the next one.

## Output
Cart: item, size, price, store, running total vs budget, fees, and the swaps you made. Count the spend toward the month's Groceries line (see jake-budget-check).
